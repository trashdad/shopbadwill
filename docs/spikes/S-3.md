# S-3 · Soft-close verdict (T-09)

**Question:** does an SGW auction's `endTime` move when someone bids in its last seconds?
**Status:** observed 2026-10-08 (PT, 01:29-02:39), anonymous, **7 of 12 requests** used.

## Verdict

**`inconclusive`** by the pre-set rule (hard-close needs late bids on at least 2 items; only 1 occurred). The evidence points to `hard-close`:

- Item E took a bid **6.833 s** before its original end; its `endTime` did not move and it closed at the original end.
- S-1 (T-07, requests #5 and #12) holds one more such point: a last bid **5.84 s** before `endTime`, `endTime` unchanged after close.
- Two independent items, two bids inside the last 7 s, zero extensions. A soft-close window shorter than about 5 s is not excluded; a window of 7 s or more is, for these two items.

**Recommended `ASSUME_EXTENSION_MS = 0`.** The SnipeEngine (Phase 4) must keep the `extended` outcome path regardless: if a read ever shows `endTime` later than the planned one, or a bid time after the original end, the engine takes that path instead of assuming the auction closed. This spike creates no engine files; T-80 reads the constant from here.

**Evidence that would settle it:** at least 2 more items, each with a bid inside the last ~10 s (ideally also one after the original end). Use fresh anonymous reads, no polling near close: a pre-read at least 10 min before the end and a post-read at least 10 min after. The user's own first live snipe (dry-run and real) will also show it: a `bidHistory` time within the last seconds with a moved `endTime` would flip this to `soft-close`.

## Method (design A)

- **One search** (`Search/ItemListing`, ending soonest, page 1, 40 rows, no keyword) at 08:29 UTC. Each chosen row's `endTime` is that item's `endTimeAtFirstRead`. Rows ending 12 min or more after the search qualified (the brief needs 10 min; 2 min margin). Of 40 rows: 28 had fewer than 3 bids, 4 ended too soon, 6 chosen, 2 spare.
- **Precision check:** every chosen row `endTime` was whole-second, and so was every post-read `endTime`. They compare exactly.
- **One ItemDetail read per item**, 10.5-10.7 min after its original end. Nothing was sent between the search and the first post-read (no polling near close). Bid times are ms precision.
- Spacing end-to-start was at least 120 s for every pair (closest: 130 s start-to-start for C after B, with the 0.4 s reply in between), and the T-07 log's last request was about 1 h earlier. Anonymous: no cookie, no Authorization, the same headless Chromium UA as T-07. All 7 replies were 200.
- Items were labelled A-F in end order. Raw replies are in the git-ignored `test/fixtures/sgw/raw/softclose/`; this doc carries no ids, titles or names.

## Notes

- Bids were not equally likely to be late: the search ranks by end time, not by bid count, and a quiet overnight window (about 01:30-02:20 PT) had few contested items. A busier evening window would likely produce the late bids needed.
- `bidHistory.bidComplete` and `bidSummary` agree on the last bid time (bid times keep ms and trim trailing zeros, as in S-1).
- Reproduce: `pnpm exec tsx scripts/softclose-observe.ts search`, then `observe`, then `report` (offline). Pure parts are tested in `test/unit/scripts/softclose-observe.test.ts`.
